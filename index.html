Mixing the vial

  1. Wash your hands and clear a clean surface. Let both vials sit out for a few minutes so they are near room temperature.
  2. Pop the caps and wipe both rubber tops with a fresh alcohol swab. Let them air dry.
  3. With a new syringe, pull 3 mL of bacteriostatic water. A 1 mL insulin syringe holds 1 mL per fill, so this takes 3 fills. Never touch the needle or let it touch anything but the rubber tops.
  4. Push the needle into the peptide vial at an angle and let the water run slowly down the glass wall. Do not spray it straight onto the powder.
  5. Gently swirl or roll the vial between your palms. Do not shake it. Let it sit until the liquid is fully clear with no flakes.
  6. Write the date on the vial, tap Start new vial above, and store it in the fridge (2 to 8°C). Never freeze it and keep it out of light.

Drawing a dose

  1. Wash your hands. Check the liquid is clear and the vial is not past its discard date.
  2. Wipe the rubber top with an alcohol swab and let it dry.
  3. Take a new U-100 syringe and pull air into it up to your line: 50 units.
  4. Push the needle into the vial and inject that air. This keeps the pressure even so it draws smoothly.
  5. Flip the vial upside down with the needle tip under the liquid and slowly pull back to 50 units.
  6. Tap the syringe to float any bubbles up, push them out, and top back up to the line if needed.
  7. Read the line at the top flat edge of the black stopper, the side closest to the needle.
  8. After injecting, put the syringe straight into a sharps container and tap I took my dose. Never reuse or share syringes.
